costlint is licensed under the [PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

**You can use it free** if you are an individual, a non-commercial user, or a company that, in the prior tax year,
had fewer than 100 people working as employees and independent contractors and less than US$1,000,000 (2019
dollars) in total revenue. The license text is the authority on what qualifies.

**Larger organisations need a commercial license** to use costlint for their benefit, including in CI on their
repositories. A commercial license covers use across the organisation and includes priority support for rule
false positives.
